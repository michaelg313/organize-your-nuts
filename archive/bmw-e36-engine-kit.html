Connection A — Exhaust Manifold to Downpipe
Manifold Flange Fasteners
12 studs and 12 copper locking nuts securing the exhaust manifold to the downpipe flange.
Single-Use Hardware — Do Not Reuse. The M7 copper locking nuts (BMW OEM #11127968) are one-time-use fasteners. The copper deforms on torque to create a gas-tight seal. Reusing these nuts risks exhaust leaks and stud pull-out. Always install new nuts every time the downpipe is removed.
